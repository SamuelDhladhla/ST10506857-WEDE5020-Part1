# Part 3 SEO Plan

## On-page SEO
- Unique `<title>` and meta description on every page.
- Relevant page-specific keywords.
- One clear H1 per page and logical H2/H3 structure.
- Descriptive alternative text on images.
- Internal links between related pages.
- Descriptive lowercase filenames and URLs.
- Responsive, mobile-friendly layouts.
- Responsive images, lazy loading and explicit image dimensions.
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata for social sharing.
- LocalBusiness/CafeOrCoffeeShop structured data on the Home page.

## Technical SEO
- `robots.txt`.
- `sitemap.xml`.
- Deferred JavaScript.
- Optimised responsive image variants.
- Semantic HTML5.
- Accessibility and focus states.

## Off-page SEO strategy
- Share the deployed site on social media.
- Encourage mentions and backlinks from local directories, community pages and relevant café/food blogs.
- Create and optimise a Google Business Profile if this were a real organisation.
- Keep the business name, address and phone number consistent across platforms.

## Local SEO
The content uses Johannesburg and Gauteng location terms where appropriate. A Google Maps embed is included on the Contact page.

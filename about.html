About Oak & Bean Coffee

Oak & Bean Coffee is a proposed neighbourhood café created as part of the WEDE5020 academic project. The concept focuses on quality coffee, fresh café food and a warm environment where customers can meet, work or socialise.

Our Story

The idea behind Oak & Bean Coffee is simple: create a café that feels dependable and welcoming. Instead of trying to offer everything, the business focuses on a thoughtful menu, friendly service and a comfortable atmosphere.

The name combines the strength and warmth associated with an oak tree with the coffee bean at the heart of the café experience.

Mission and Vision

Mission

To provide quality coffee, fresh food and friendly service in a relaxed neighbourhood environment.

Vision

To become a trusted local café known for consistency, community and a comfortable customer experience.

Our Values

  • Quality: Serve products we are proud to offer.
  • Welcome: Make every visitor feel comfortable and respected.
  • Consistency: Deliver a dependable experience each time customers visit.
  • Community: Create a space where people can meet and connect.

Who We Serve

Oak & Bean Coffee is designed for students, young professionals, remote workers, local residents and visitors looking for a calm and convenient café experience.

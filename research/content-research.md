# Oak & Bean Coffee — Content Research and Sourcing

Oak & Bean Coffee is a fictional small business created for the WEDE5020 POE.

The organisation story, mission, vision, menu descriptions, address, contact details and page copy are original academic content created specifically for the website.

## Content by page
- Home: introduction, offering, about preview, visit information.
- About: story, mission, vision and values.
- Menu: coffee, light meals and baked goods with realistic sample prices.
- Enquiry: accessible form for general, group and booking enquiries.
- Contact: contact information, opening hours and directions.

Technical references used for HTML, CSS, responsive design and GitHub are listed in the main README.
